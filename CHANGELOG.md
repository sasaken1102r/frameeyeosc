# Changelog

## 0.8.2-beta.5 (2026-10-27)

The eye preview's camera pictures now show where the eye cameras found your eyelids, as two dots above and below the pupil, and switches let you hide each mark. How the eyes are read and sent doesn't change.

日本語: 目のプレビューのカメラ映像に、目のカメラが見つけたまぶたの位置を瞳の上と下の点で出すようにしました。映像に重ねる印はスイッチでそれぞれ消せます。目の読み取り方や送る値は変わりません。

- Panel, eye preview with the camera picture on: the picture now also shows where the eye cameras found your eyelids, a light blue dot on the upper lid's edge and a pink one on the lower lid, above and below the pupil, and the camera's eyelid value at its bottom left ("Camera lid 0.62"). A dot is left out on frames where that lid wasn't found. The dots and the value are smoothed so they don't flicker; with "Raw" they are drawn as read. Nothing about how the eyes are read changes.
- Panel, eye preview with the camera picture on: switches between the two pictures turn each mark on them on or off: "Eyelids" (both lid dots and the camera's eyelid value), "Pupil" (the green circle) and "Search area" (the yellow frame). They start on each time the panel starts. "Read as closed" always shows.

## 0.8.2-beta.4 (2026-10-26)

With prescription inserts, a wide-open eye could still be read as half-shut by the eye cameras; widening should now show more reliably (calibrate the cameras again after updating). The "Half-shut check (AI)" was retrained so that a bright band or patch in the eye picture no longer makes it cancel closes and squints.

日本語: 度付きインサートを使っていると、大きく見開いた目が目のカメラで半目に読まれることがまだありました。見開きがもっと安定して出るはずです（更新したらカメラの校正をやり直してね）。「半目の読み直し（AI）」を学習し直して、目の映像に明るい帯や明るいところがあっても、閉じや細めを取り消さないようにしました。

- eyecam, widening: with the pupil seen whole, an upper lid line found less than 1.2 times the pupil's minor radius above its centre (0.5 times before) is now searched for again above the pupil. On a wide-open eye the first line can sit on the pupil's own blurred top edge, 1.05 to 1.21 times that radius above its centre, and the widened eye read half-shut. On two recordings from someone wearing prescription inserts, widen frames read half-shut went from 37.1 % / 15.8 % (left / right) to 5.0 % / 3.6 %, and widening was caught in 91.7 % of them instead of 59.2 %; closes and squints stayed the same. On the developer's 26 recordings and 10 calibrations, every measure (half-shut, squint, widening, blinks) stayed the same. Run "Calibrate the cameras (18 s)" again after updating.
- eyecam, "Half-shut check (AI)" (off by default): the network's weights were retrained on the developer's recordings with white bands, dimming and shifts of up to 10 px drawn in. The earlier weights read a closed eye as open in pictures with a bright band or patch: on one wearer with prescription inserts they cancelled 14 % of closes and half of the squints. Now they cancel none on that recording, nor on the developer's recordings with a band over 35 % of the picture or dimmed with a band. Open eyes read half-shut on the developer's recordings go from 2.98 % to 3.11 % (each day left out of training in turn); blinks and squints stay the same. With the check off, nothing changes.

## 0.8.2-beta.3 (2026-10-25)

New in the panel: "Record for squint training" (Advanced › Debug tools, once the eye cameras are set up) records your eyes for the developer with one press, to train the "Half-shut check (AI)" on more people's eyes. It is entirely optional: it says first what is recorded and how it is used, and records only after you agree. Widening is also easier to reach for people whose upper lid reads at a different height in each widen of the eye-camera calibration (calibrate the cameras again after updating). With the headset off, the eye cameras could also be locked onto only part of their slots, leaving one eye slow or sent the other eye's frames; that is fixed.

日本語: パネルに「細めの学習用に撮る」を足しました（詳細 › 動作確認。目のカメラの準備が済むと使えます）。「半目の読み直し（AI）」をいろんな人の目で学習させるための録画を、ボタン 1 つで撮って開発者に送れます。協力するかは自由で、何を録ってどう使うかを先に見せて、同意したときだけ撮ります。また、目のカメラの校正で見開くたびに上まぶたの高さが違って読まれる人も、見開きが届きやすくなりました（更新したらカメラの校正をやり直してね）。HMD を外しているあいだに目のカメラのつかみ方が偏って、片目が遅くなったり、もう片方の目の映像が送られたりすることがあったのも直しました。

- "Record for squint training": the first time (and when the consent's text changes) a screen says what is recorded (eye-camera video, about 2.7 GB each, staying on the Frame until you send it), how it is used (only to tune the squint and half-shut check and train its AI; only the trained AI ships in the app, never the video; kept on the developer's own PCs; deleted on request, though not out of an AI already released) and how to send it (a private share link from Google Drive or similar, never in a GitHub issue or anywhere public), then asks whether glasses or prescription inserts are worn. Then one recording of eyecam's new `protocol_contrib.txt` (about 93 s, no full-view light: 10 s relaxed, closing, squinting as in bright light and widening three times, looking up, down, left and right).
- The recordings gather in `~/eyecam/contrib/`, each moved there once it can be used for training: `eye_L.raw`, `eye_R.raw`, `frames.csv`, `valve.csv`, `cues.csv` and `meta.txt` there, frames of both eyes, each raw file exactly as many frames as `frames.csv` lists for that eye (one cut short when the disk ran full is caught), and Valve's tracking values near at least 90 % of the frames; checked from the CSV files and file sizes alone, again when packing. One that can't be used, or stopped early, goes to `aborted/` with why, to be recorded again. Beside each go copies of the newest calibration result, the user calibration (`calib.json`) and eyecam's settings then. `info.txt` there gets a section for each: the app's and eyecam-rec's versions, the eyelid veto and widening settings, the answer about glasses, the minutes since the one before and whether the headset was taken off in between; no personal data.
- A recording where one eye's camera delivered under 90 % of the other's frames (a ring locked on part of its slots: a quarter, a half or three quarters) is kept out too, asking to put the headset on again and record again.
- "Start recording" waits for frameeyeosc to run with eye data coming in (Valve's values, which the labels need, only come while it asks for them), and says so.
- The end's screen (also from "Recordings") shows how many there are and their size, the folder, how to send it, "Make one file" (a `.tar.zst` through `zstd -6`, measured on the Frame's eye video at 0.58 of the size, which 7-Zip and the like open; a `.tar` without zstd; packed in the background at a low priority after checking the free space; the recordings stay) and "Delete the recordings" (after a confirmation).
- eyecam: new `protocol_contrib.txt`; new beeps for `look_left` (2 long, high then low) and `look_right` (2 long, low then high), which had the squint's beep; status.json's new `headset_off_count` (the times the headset was taken off for 10 s or more and worn again since eyecam-rec started, by the eye video stopping or the proximity sensor, on a clock that counts suspend) and `started_unix`.
- `frameeyeosc-panel --dump-png … --fake-contrib STATE` draws each of its screens.
- eyecam, widening: the wear calibration's widen level is now the lowest of the widen steps' own medians (only steps with 30 or more frames with the lid line), not the median over all of them. When one widen step read the upper lid much higher than the others, the widen step came out too large for the widening the others reached, and widening was hard to reach: on one such recording (with prescription inserts) the left eye's step went from 0.52 to 0.25, and widening went out at 0.5 or more in 91 % of the widen frames instead of 36-39 %, while the normal and squint steps stayed unwidened; on the developer's 27 wear calibrations the step stayed the same or got slightly smaller (by 0.011 in the median). `calib_result.json` now also has each eye's `widen_step_levels` (each widen step's median). Run "Calibrate the cameras (18 s)" again for it to take effect.
- eyecam, eye cameras: with the headset off, eyecam could lock onto only part of the cameras' slots, so one eye was read at a quarter to three quarters of its frame rate (the right eye at 22.5 of 90 fps, through a calibration and a recording), or, with one camera's slots alone, the right eye was sent the left eye's frames. It happened in 17 of 138 locks over ten days on the developer's headset, always with the headset off or just put on. eyecam now locks only when each camera has all four of its slots, never splits one camera's slots into two eyes by their pictures, and, as a safety net, looks for the cameras again when one camera's frames come at under half the other's for 5 s (only once for the same slots). A camera that stops altogether is handled as before.

## 0.8.2-beta.2 (2026-10-24)

The gaze no longer stays frozen when SteamOS keeps reading one open eye as closed: after a second, the other eye moves the gaze (#32). With "Move eyes separately" on, the new "Own gaze per eye" (Gaze › Reach) lets each eye's own sideways gaze through, so looking inward with both eyes can show. The eye cameras no longer lose a pupil that suddenly looks larger, the camera eyelids no longer take over for good after many blinks on SteamOS 0.4.x, and a new user calibration now sets the squint at the same depth whatever the wear. With prescription inserts, the eye cameras should find the upper eyelid more reliably, so widening can show and an open eye is less often read as a squint.

日本語: SteamOS が開いた片目を閉じていると読み続けても、視線が止まったままにならなくなりました。1 秒たつと、もう片方の目で視線を動かします（#32）。「左右の目を別々に動かす」がオンのとき、視線 › 動く範囲の新しい「目ごとの視線」で、目ごとの左右の視線をそのまま使えるようになりました（両目で内側を見るのも出せます）。目のカメラは、急に大きく写った瞳を見失わなくなりました。SteamOS 0.4.x で、まばたきを重ねるうちにまぶたがカメラ側に切り替わったままになることもなくなりました。新しくユーザー校正をすると、かぶるたびに細めの深さが変わることもなくなります。度付きインサートを使っているときも、目のカメラが上まぶたを見つけやすくなるはずです。見開きが出るようになり、開いた目が細めに読まれることも減ります。

- Gaze: the gaze is held while either eye's openness is below `gaze_hold_below` (0.5), for blinks. SteamOS can read one open eye at about 0.06 for minutes to hours (with prescription inserts, for example), and the gaze stood still all that time while the eyelids and pupils kept moving. Now one eye alone below the line holds the gaze for up to 1 s (a wink); longer, and that eye's reading is taken as wrong: it no longer holds the gaze, and the other eye moves both. Blinks are held as before, and an eye read closed for good is not held again for a second after each blink. A single sample read open doesn't start the second over.
- New setting `own_eye_gaze` (0 by default, 0 to 1; `--own-eye-gaze`), "Own gaze per eye" on Gaze › Reach under "Move eyes separately" (0-100 %, 5 % steps). With "Move eyes separately" on (v2), it blends each eye's sideways gaze from the shared one turned in by 2° (0 %, as before) to each eye's own sideways gaze as the Frame tracks it (100 %). The Frame's per-eye sideways gaze wobbles, so the avatar's eyes may twitch at higher values. Up/down, the eyelids and everything else stay as they are; an eye read closed for long (above) or with an unreliable gaze still takes the other's. At 0 everything sent is as before. It is greyed out while "Move eyes separately" is off, and in v1 (which already sends each eye's own gaze).
- eyecam: a pupil that suddenly looks more than 1.3 times as large as usual (looking far toward your ear turns it toward the camera; a darker world dilates it) was dropped by the size check right after a blink, and stayed lost until it shrank again (7.4 s in one recording). Now, when such a pupil is as dark and as whole as the open one and no flatter, and is seen for 0.3 s, it is taken and the usual size starts over from it. Replayed on 26 recordings: the pupil found in 89.0 % of open-eye frames instead of 88.8 %, closes missed 0.386 instead of 0.402 a minute, closing, squint and widening the same.
- Eyelids with the eye cameras on SteamOS 0.4.x: its eye tracker often reads an open eye well below the camera, so the two never agreed and each blink's camera lag added to the eyelid's "doubt" until the camera drove the eyelid for good, and blinks the camera missed went out open. The doubt now starts over once the eye tracker has read the eye open for 1 s. In replays the right eye's time on the camera eyelid went from 130 s to 111 s in 0.67 h; 0.3-0.6 % of eyelid samples changed.
- eyecam, user calibration: the squint was saved as a fraction of that wear's open-to-closed gap but used against the usual gap, which differs by 0.6 to 1.5 times from wear to wear, so the calibrated squint came out 0.65 to 1.5 times as deep. The user calibration now also saves the squint's depth (`sq_depth` in each eye's user values), and live uses it, kept within the same limits as the calibration's. Open eyes read as a squint went from 2.4 % to 0.8 % in the guided normal step; a full squint reads a little lower (0.9 or more 35 % of the time instead of 53 %). This takes effect after a new user calibration; older calibrations work as before. If your squint now reads too shallow, run the user calibration again. Going back to an older version and saving a calibration there drops `sq_depth`, so run the user calibration again after updating.
- eyecam, prescription inserts: with an insert in front of the eye cameras, a closed eye could read partly open, and an iris fit far too large made closes harder to see. A lower lid line found on a closed eye 0.9 iris radii or more below where it was with the eye open, or against the bottom of its search band, is no longer taken: the last one seen with the pupil is used instead (the lower lid hardly moves in a blink), and an upper lid line below the centre of a pupil seen whole is dropped. Re-fitting one such wear calibration's samples, the left eye's open-to-closed gap goes from 0.30 to 1.35 (its right eye: 1.31). When a wear's iris radius comes out over 4 times the open pupil's, the iris edge is taken at the first rise out of the iris instead of the strongest edge, and the radius and the open eye's iris-box level are learnt again. Replayed on 26 recordings with a band added that pulls the radius from 61 / 54 px to 79 / 75 px: 72 / 67 px, closes missed 1.08 instead of 1.27 a minute, blinks under 0.2 s caught 83 % instead of 80 %, but open eyes read half-shut 6.4 % of the time instead of 5.3 %. On the original recordings the radius switch never happens; guided closes were caught 98.2 % of the time instead of 97.3 %, and everything else stayed about the same (open eyes read half-shut 5.4 % of the time instead of 5.3 %). If you use a prescription insert, run "Calibrate the cameras (18 s)" again after updating.
- eyecam, prescription inserts and widening: with a brightly lit white of the eye and glints from an insert, the upper lid line was often taken at the bright area's upper edge, under or just above the pupil's centre, and the wear calibration's widen level at the tips of long upper lashes, so the widen step came out 3 to 4 times the usual size and widening could never be reached, while open eyes read as a squint. Now, when the pupil is seen whole and the line lies below the upper half of the pupil, the lid is searched for again above the pupil, and that line is taken if it is at least as clear. When the widen level comes from the higher search area and that area also has a line in the normal step, the widen step is how far that line rose. A widen step above 1.1 iris radii is taken as a widen not caught (the usual step is used), and steps above 0.9 already saved are left out of the history's median. Replaying one such wearer's recording, the widen step went from 1.30 / 1.15 to 0.52 / 0.51, and with a user calibration made from that same recording (so a favourable test) the right eye read as widened in about 90 % of its widen frames instead of none (the left eye 19 to 76 % instead of 0 to 35 %), and the squint in the normal step went from 0.16-0.63 to 0-0.07. The left eye's widened line often sits at the top of the iris, so its widening may still read a little low. On the developer's recordings, the calibrations came out the same, and 0.16 % of the samples in one of 19 recordings changed. If you use a prescription insert, run "Calibrate the cameras (18 s)" again after updating.
- eyecam: when the eye cameras locked on while the headset was off (after a restart, say), they learnt the empty headset's picture (where to look for the pupil, its brightness), and kept it once the headset was put on if the video paused for less than 10 s; the next camera calibrations then found the pupil in only 5 to 162 of 486 frames. The headset now also counts as put on again when the eye video comes back after a pause, however short, and when the proximity sensor says worn after 10 s of not worn, unless an eye still looks worn (its pupil seen in at least a fifth of the last 10 s of video, at an iris size close to your usual one; the sensor can read low for a second or two while worn), and never during a calibration. A fresh start found the pupil in all 486 frames of that calibration.
- Panel: while the eye cameras calibrate, the gaze re-center and the eye fit no longer start. A re-center asked for by another app (a double tap in frame-aux-shortcuts, say) during a user calibration showed its dot and saved the gaze center measured while you were looking up for the calibration. `--action recenter`, `fit` and `relearn` now exit with 11 and "calibrating" in their line, "Fit my eyes" and "Re-center only" are grey meanwhile, and the automatic re-center after putting the headset on waits for the calibration to end.
- Logs: the "Eye data has been low" line said "-0 published samples were missed" when none were; it now says 0.

With a glare band, the eyelids may read a little half shut more often while you look toward your ear (to be tuned in a later version).
- Test versions: with "Get test versions" turned off on a test version, Advanced › Version offers the regular version when it is older than yours ("You can go back to the regular version 0.8.0" and "Go back"). It asks first, then installs it like an update; there's no notice in the left column, and nothing changes unless you press it. Until now the test version stayed until the next regular version came out. The shared updater is frame-update 0.5.0.

## 0.8.0 (2026-10-18)

Your avatar's brows can now go up when you widen your eyes (Eyelids › Brows: "Widen raises brows", off by default). The eye cameras don't see brows, so the brows follow the widening, also with Widening off. They go to VRChat directly, through LiveLink and through the FrameEyeOSC module, whose version 1.0.1 already reads them, so the module needs no update. With the eye cameras, the new "Half-shut check (AI)" on Eyelids › Look (off by default, experimental) lets a small AI undo an open eye read as half shut, for example while looking to the side. The user calibration no longer fails when only the look-down step can't be measured on an eye (long lashes, for example): the squint is saved, and that eye keeps its previous look-down correction (or uses the standard one if it had none).

日本語: 目を見開くと、アバターの眉も上げられるようになりました（まぶた ›「眉」の「見開きで眉も上げる」。既定はしない）。目のカメラは眉を見ていないので、眉は見開きについていきます。「見開き」が「しない」でも上がります。VRChat に直接・LiveLink・FrameEyeOSC モジュールのどれにも届き、モジュールは 1.0.1 のままで読めるので、入れ直しは要りません。目のカメラでは、まぶた ›「見た目」に「半目の読み直し（AI）」を足しました（既定はしない。まだ試しの機能です）。横を見たときなどに開いた目が半目に読まれたら、小さな AI が見直して開いた目に戻します。ユーザー校正は、下を見る段だけが測れなかった目（まつげが長いときなど）があっても失敗しなくなりました。細めは覚えて、その目は下を見たときの補正に標準の値を使います。

- New setting `brow_from_widen` (off by default; `--brow-from-widen`) with `brow_strength` (0.8 by default, 0 to 1; `--brow-strength`), on the Eyelids tab's new fourth page "Brows": "Widen raises brows" On / Off and, while it is on, "Brow raise strength" (Weak to Strong). The eye cameras don't see brows, so the brows follow the widening: an eye widened past 15 % raises its brow, up to `brow_strength` at full widening, also with Widening off. A closing or closed eye keeps its brow (a blink doesn't drop it), and in a wink the closed eye's brow follows the open one's. Brows are never lowered. With it off, everything sent is byte for byte as before.
- Where the brows go: VRChat directly gets VRCFT's v2 brow parameters (`BrowExpressionLeft` / `Right` / `BrowExpression`, `BrowUpLeft` / `Right` / `BrowUp`, `BrowInnerUp`, `BrowOuterUp`), all set to 0 once when the brows stop (turned off, the eye data stops, sending stops or the output changes). LiveLink sends `BrowInnerUp` and `BrowOuterUpLeft` / `Right`. FrameEyeOSC sends 108-byte packets with the brows block (`docs/protocol.md`); the FrameEyeOSC module 1.0.1 already reads it, so the module needs no update. ETVR gets no brows. It works on avatars with brow parameters.
- Eye preview: while brows are sent, a brow is drawn over each eye at the value sent. It isn't drawn for the raw or camera values, since brows aren't measured. The panel gets the brows in the same 160-byte datagram as before, in bytes that were reserved. Without brows the preview looks exactly as before.
- Eye cameras, "Half-shut check (AI)" On / Off on Eyelids › Look (off by default; shown while the eye cameras are in use, with the green "Camera" badge): eyecam's new eyelid veto. Looking far to the side, the lid and lashes cover the pupil, and an open eye can read as half shut. Only while eyecam reads an eye below 0.5 (or closed), a small CNN looks at that eye's camera picture; once it is sure the eye is open for 3 frames in a row (10 while closed), eyecam reads the eye at least relaxed open, with no squint. Replayed on 26 recordings, open eyes read as half shut in free use went from 5.5 % to 2.9 % of the time, and looking more than 20° to the side toward the ear from 18.2 % to 1.4 %; closing, squint, widening and blinks came out the same. One run takes about 0.6-0.75 ms on the Frame (one core). With the user calibration it may also open up a light squint. Experimental: it was trained on the developer's eyes only. The switch is eyecam's `lid_veto` (saved in `~/.config/eyecam/settings.json`); eyecam's control socket has the new command `set lid_veto on|off`, and its status.json gains `lid_veto` (on or off, runs a second, how often it stepped in, time per run).
- eyecam, user calibration: with long lashes the lid line can sit on the lashes, so looking down hardly changes the eye's opening, and the look-down step always failed; no user calibration was saved, and the squint stayed off. Now, when an eye's squint was measured and only its look-down correction wasn't (looking down didn't narrow the eye below 0.95 of normal, the lower lid moved up, or too few frames had a lid line), the calibration succeeds: the squint is saved, and that eye keeps the look-down correction of its previous user calibration, or, if it had none, uses the standard one, the same as without a user calibration. The message names the eye ("Calibrated (Right eye: keeping the previous correction for looking down)" or "... using the standard correction for looking down"), and the panel's result card shows a "Partly previous" or "Partly standard" pill and says which eye. The left/right difference warning then compares only the squint. An eye whose squint wasn't measured, or whose pupil wasn't seen, still fails as before. eyecam's status.json gains `user_look_down_default` and `user_look_down_previous` [left, right], and calib_result.json `look_down_default` and `look_down_previous`.
- eyecam, user calibration: a squint narrower than the closed eye (`f_sq` below -0.5, where the squint reaches only 0.67 before the eye reads closed) now fails that eye's squint step with "the squint was narrower than the closed eye (try again without closing it)". The 19 user calibrations that passed before had `f_sq` from -0.25 to 0.71 and come out the same.

## 0.7.10 (2026-10-17)

Other apps on the Frame can now press the panel's eye fit buttons and the new "Relearn my eyes" with `frameeyeosc-panel --action`; frame-aux-shortcuts, my app for shortcuts on the aux button, can use it to put a re-center or an eye fit on a press of that button. "Relearn my eyes" on the Eye cameras tab learns your relaxed eyes again in about 30 s without a calibration, and the eyelids, widening and squint keep working from the old values meanwhile. Widening can now be turned off altogether (Eyelids › Widening: Off). With the eye cameras, eyecam no longer reads a widened eye as almost shut when the upper lid goes above the area it searches, and the wear calibration measures widening for wearers whose lid goes up there. ## 0.7.1 (2026-10-03)

Eye data at the full rate while Steam Link streams, no stray widening on SteamOS 0.4.3, and two opt-ins for avatars not made for VRCFaceTracking: Steam Link's parameter names and VRChat's own eye tracking.

日本語: Steam Link で配信中でも目のデータが全部届くように。SteamOS 0.4.3 で勝手に見開かないように。VRCFaceTracking 用じゃないアバター向けに、Steam Link の名前で送る機能と、VRChat 標準の目も動かす機能を追加。

- The eye data no longer drops to about half (46 a second instead of 90) when reading, processing and sending one sample takes longer than a frame (11.1 ms), as can happen while Steam Link's video fills the Wi-Fi. The eye tracker publishes a frame only if a sample was asked for by then, and clears the request with it (read off its own code). frameeyeosc asked for the next sample only once it had finished with the last one, so whenever that took longer than a frame, the eye tracker skipped the next one. It now asks for the next sample in the same lock that copies the current one out, and reads a sample published while it was busy at once instead of waiting for the one after. It still touches only the lock, the sequence and the request flag. In a test with a stand-in eye tracker that publishes 90 frames a second only when asked and wakes the reader 8 ms late, and a reader that takes 7 ms over each sample, 45 of 90 frames were read before and 90 of 90 now. The sockets don't block any more, so a datagram the network can't take at once (its send buffer is full) is dropped, not waited for, and the eye data keeps coming in. Drops aren't "sending failed"; the log says how many at most once a minute (`Dropped … datagrams to …`).

## 0.4.0 (2026-09-27)

Steadier eyes and fewer missed blinks. The ideas come from the README of [CyrusOtter/SteamFrameEye](https://github.com/CyrusOtter/SteamFrameEye).

- A closed eye is sent fully closed for at least 80 ms (`blink_hold_ms`), so short blinks show up for other players. Longer blinks keep their own length.
- One-sample glitches in gaze and eyelids are removed (`despike`). Everything arrives one sample (~11 ms) later.
- When one eye is closed and the other nearly so, both are sent closed (`blink_sync_below`). Winks pass through.
- Optional, off by default: an eye whose gaze the Frame itself marks as uncertain (`gaze_quality_limit`) can be kept from moving the gaze. The other eye moves both, or the gaze is held while both are uncertain, and eyelid calibration doesn't learn meanwhile. It's a safety net. On a well-fitted headset it measured no benefit, because the uncertainty only rises while the eyes are mostly shut, where the gaze is already held.
- The panel has the four new settings: the quality check and glitch removal on the Gaze page, and the blink hold and closing both eyes in one row on the Eyelids page.
- The gaze deadzone default is now 0.02 (0.9°) instead of 0.03 (1.35°), so small gaze shifts come through.
- Gaps in eye tracking are logged to the journal: one line when samples stop, with the reason (the eye server stopped producing, no new samples for 1 s, or an unreadable sample), and one when they come back ("Eye tracking resumed after 5.2 s"). When the eye tracker restarts and re-creates its shared memory, frameeyeosc reattaches and logs it. If the new one isn't ready yet, it keeps retrying instead of exiting.
- `--record FILE` writes the eye tracker's raw samples to a CSV file, and `--replay FILE` runs such a file through the processing and prints how the output behaved, next to the same settings with the steps above turned off.
- The panel shows the installed version on the Advanced page and can update frameeyeosc: it asks GitHub for a newer release at start and at most once a day ("Check for updates", `update_check`, on by default), "Check now" asks right away, and "Update" installs the new release after one confirmation. Downloads are checked against the release's `SHA256SUMS`, a checksum from the same release (not a signature) that catches a corrupted or incomplete download. Releases without it are left for a manual update. After a failed check, the panel tries again an hour later. While a new release is available, a notice at the bottom of the left column leads to it.
- `install.sh` also installs the update script (`~/.local/share/frameeyeosc/frame-update.sh`) and remembers its options in `~/.config/frameeyeosc/install-args`, so an update from the panel installs the same way. Versions before 0.4.0 have no updater, so update to 0.4.0 once by hand with `./install.sh --with-panel` as before. From 0.4.0 on, the panel's "Update" button does it. If `install-args` is missing, the panel's update installs with `--with-panel`.
- `scripts/package.sh` writes `dist/SHA256SUMS` next to the tarball; attach both to the release.

## 0.2.0 (2026-09-26)

First release of this fork, based on [konsti219/frameeyeosc](https://github.com/konsti219/frameeyeosc) at `b9f0c01`.

- OSC now goes to the PC that Steam Link is streaming from. `--target auto` is the new default; it used to be `127.0.0.1:9000`.
- Gaze and eyelids go through One Euro filters. Gaze also has a small deadzone and stays put while the eyes are shut.
- Both eyes get the same gaze by default. Use `--independent-eyes` for per-eye gaze.
- Eyelid openness is converted to the VRCFT scale (closed, relaxed, widened), with a deadzone so widening doesn't trigger too easily.
- Each eye's relaxed openness is learned and saved, so small left/right differences even out. Winks still come through.
- It can run as a systemd user service that starts with SteamVR and restarts if it fails. When the eye tracker restarts, it reopens the shared memory.
- Added `install.sh`, which needs no sudo, and `scripts/package.sh` for building release tarballs.
